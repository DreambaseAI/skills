# Sources, Provenance, and Reuse Policy

This skill is an original synthesis of public guidance and research. It does not reproduce source figures, screenshots, full tables, proprietary templates, or long passages.

## Reuse rules

- Treat publicly accessible material as copyrighted unless its license says otherwise.
- Use ideas, principles, methods, and factual findings in original wording.
- Cite the source when a recommendation is based on a named framework or publication.
- Do not bundle source PDFs or images into the skill without permission or a compatible license.
- Do not imply endorsement by any cited author or organization.
- Review the current license before redistributing adapted source assets.

## Foundational perception and design (see foundations.md)

1. **Jacques Bertin**, *Semiology of Graphics* (1967; English 1983, Esri reissue 2010) — the visual variables and their levels of organization; origin of encoding channels.
2. **William Cleveland & Robert McGill**, "Graphical Perception" (*JASA* 79:387, 1984) — https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01621459.1984.10478080 — experimental accuracy ranking of perceptual tasks.
3. **Jock Mackinlay**, "Automating the Design of Graphical Presentations" (*ACM TOG*, 1986) — effectiveness rankings per data type.
4. **Jeffrey Heer & Michael Bostock**, "Crowdsourcing Graphical Perception" (*CHI 2010*) — https://idl.uw.edu/papers/crowdsourcing-graphical-perception — web-scale replication; chart-height and gridline-spacing findings.
5. **Edward Tufte**, *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information* (Graphics Press, 1983/2001) — https://www.edwardtufte.com/book/the-visual-display-of-quantitative-information/ — data-ink ratio, chartjunk, lie factor, small multiples, graphical integrity.
6. **Ben Shneiderman**, "The Eyes Have It" (*IEEE VL 1996*) — https://www.cs.umd.edu/~ben/papers/Shneiderman1996eyes.pdf — the information-seeking mantra and task×data-type taxonomy.
7. **Tamara Munzner**, "A Nested Model for Visualization Design and Validation" (*IEEE TVCG*, 2009) — https://www.cs.ubc.ca/labs/imager/tr/2009/NestedModel/ — and *Visualization Analysis and Design* (CRC, 2014) — https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~tmm/vadbook/ — nested model, what-why-how, modern channel rankings.
8. **Hadley Wickham**, "A Layered Grammar of Graphics" — https://vita.had.co.nz/papers/layered-grammar.pdf — compositional model of mappings, marks, transforms, scales, facets.

## Practitioner guidance

9. **Stephen Few**, *Common Pitfalls in Dashboard Design*; *Save the Pies for Dessert*; "The Chartjunk Debate" — https://www.perceptualedge.com/ — dashboard-as-monitoring, at-a-glance goals, perceptual comparison limits, pie alternatives. Copyrighted; research input only.
10. **Financial Times Visual Journalism**, *Visual Vocabulary* — https://github.com/Financial-Times/chart-doctor/tree/main/visual-vocabulary — task-oriented chart taxonomy.
11. **UK Government Analysis Function**, *Data visualisation: charts* — https://analysisfunction.civilservice.gov.uk/policy-store/data-visualisation-charts/ — practical chart, titling, and accessibility guidance.
12. **Tableau**, *Best Practices for Effective Dashboards* — https://help.tableau.com/current/pro/desktop/en-us/dashboards_best_practices.htm — audience, display size, view limits, placement.
13. **Bach et al.**, "Dashboard Design Patterns" — https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.00757 — systematic dashboard design-choice space.

## Storytelling and infographics (see infographics.md)

14. **Alberto Cairo**, *The Functional Art* (2012), *The Truthful Art* (2016) — http://www.thefunctionalart.com/ — five qualities (truthful, functional, beautiful, insightful, enlightening); the Visualization Wheel trade-off axes.
15. **Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic**, *Storytelling with Data* (Wiley, 2015) — https://www.storytellingwithdata.com/books — context, Big Idea, clutter, attention, designer thinking, narrative structure. Copyrighted; research input only.
16. **Randy Krum**, *Cool Infographics* (Wiley, 2013) — https://coolinfographics.com/book/ — 5-second rule, three-act infographic structure, sourcing footer norms.
17. **Nathan Yau**, *Visualize This* / FlowingData — https://flowingdata.com/ — data-first workflow; visualization as the end of an analysis pipeline.
18. **Bateman et al.**, "Useful Junk?" (*CHI 2010*) — https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1753326.1753716 — embellishment effects on recall; scope limits apply.
19. **Borkin et al.**, "What Makes a Visualization Memorable?" (*IEEE TVCG*, 2013) and "Beyond Memorability" (2015) — https://vcg.seas.harvard.edu/ — memorability drivers; titles dominate recall.
20. **Nielsen Norman Group**, F-pattern and text-scanning eyetracking research — https://www.nngroup.com/articles/text-scanning-patterns-eyetracking/ — scanning is a coping behavior to design against.

## Accessibility (see color-accessibility.md)

21. **W3C**, WCAG 2.2 — https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/ — SC 1.1.1, 1.4.1, 1.4.3, 1.4.11, 1.4.13, 2.1.1, 2.4.7 as applied to charts.
22. **Frank Elavsky**, Chartability — https://chartability.fizz.studio/ — POUR+CAF framework; 50 heuristics, 14 critical. CC-BY-SA.
23. **Amy Cesal**, "Writing Alt Text for Data Visualization" (Nightingale, 2020) — https://medium.com/nightingale/writing-alt-text-for-data-visualization-2a218ef43f81 — practitioner alt-text formula.
24. **Alan Lundgard & Arvind Satyanarayan**, "Accessible Visualization via Natural Language Descriptions" (*IEEE VIS 2021*) — https://vis.csail.mit.edu/pubs/vis-text-model/ — four-level semantic model; L2/L3 content is most valued by blind readers.
25. **Datawrapper Academy**, alt-text guidance — https://www.datawrapper.de/academy/how-to-write-good-alternative-descriptions-for-your-data-visualization
26. **W3C WAI / Léonie Watson / Highcharts** — accessible SVG patterns — https://www.w3.org/wiki/SVG_Accessibility/ARIA_roles_for_charts, https://tink.uk/accessible-svg-line-graphs/, https://www.highcharts.com/docs/accessibility/accessibility-module
27. **Viz Palette** (Elijah Meeks & Susie Lu) — https://projects.susielu.com/viz-palette — CVD simulation; **ColorBrewer** (Cynthia Brewer) — https://colorbrewer2.org — CVD-safe scheme filter.
